About Me

I am a musician from Jakarta who started singing at the age of 9 and frequently participated in, and won, competitions like The Voice Kids Indonesia, FLS2N, and I Can See Your Voice Indonesia. I've also performed at various events such as Synchronize Festival, Gen on Track Live, Idea Fest by Astra, Symphonesia, Puja Indonesia with the Erwin Gutawa Orchestra, and more. I wrote the song 'Arah,' which was produced by Endah n Rhesa and Yessi Kristianto (Erwin Gutawa Orchestra), and I trained in vocals and piano at the Erwin Gutawa Music School. I was the lead vocalist of the girl band DREAMGIRLS (2021–2022) and am currently active as a content creator on Instagram, TikTok, and Likee.
